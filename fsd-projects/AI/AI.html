TACTICAL DICE

A HEROIC BATTLE SIMULATOR

⚔️ How to Play

1. Roll: You have 3 rolls per turn to get the best dice possible.

2. Lock: Click dice you want to keep. If you lock all 4, you move straight to the attack phase!

3. Assign: Once rolls are done, click a die, then click a target to assign it.

4. Execute: Hit the green Execute button to resolve the turn!
